## 3. First-3-second hook — 3 variants
Patterns: 外星人视角描述日常 / 离谱问题一本正经 / 结论先行反转 / 道具悬念(plant a prop that pays off at the end) / 直击痛点「你是不是也…」.
Each variant: spoken line (≤18 字), on-screen text, first-frame visual, beat at 0–1s and 1–3s. First frame must work as a cover.
